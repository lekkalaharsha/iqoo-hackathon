# Logic Legends — 90-second submission video

Use the generated `Logic_Legends_Explainer.mp4` as the narrated introduction.
Follow it with live phone footage if the submission permits a longer video.

## Live phone shots

1. **Eyes-free controls (8 seconds):** show the camera screen, swipe between
   modes, and let the spoken mode name remain audible.
2. **Read & Explain (20 seconds):** use a large, non-sensitive printed notice.
   Press Volume Up. Keep the raw OCR and explanation in one continuous shot.
3. **Explore (15 seconds):** photograph a book and bottle in good light. Keep
   the capture-to-description latency visible. This is the cloud prototype.
4. **Recovery (7 seconds):** press Volume Down to repeat the last answer.
5. **Voice Chat (optional):** include only if the complete turn works during
   rehearsal. Swipe to Voice Chat, tap, ask a short question, and end with
   “clear over.”

## Recording rules

- Record horizontally at 1080p with the physical phone and object visible.
- Keep device speech audible and add captions for every important spoken line.
- Never show `constapi.dart`, the API key, private messages, contacts, or location.
- Say **“cloud Gemini prototype”**. Do not claim on-device LLM inference,
  complete offline operation, medical accuracy, or blind-user validation.
- If Voice Chat fails twice, omit that shot and show Volume Up, Volume Down,
  swipe, tap, speech, and haptics instead.

## Closing line

“Logic Legends turns a phone camera into accessible understanding: hear what is
there, understand what it means, and recover without needing to see the screen.”
